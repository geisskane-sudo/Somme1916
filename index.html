1 July 1916 — the first day on the Somme

At 07:30 on Saturday 1 July 1916, after a week-long bombardment, fourteen British divisions attacked along a front from Gommecourt in the north to Maricourt in the south, next to the French. This map shows the ground as it is, with the trench lines, villages, mines and battalions of that morning.

  • Zoom out to see the whole front and the corps; zoom in to see divisions, then battalions in their trenches.
  • The clock starts at 06:00. Watch the final bombardment, the mines at 07:20 and 07:28, and zero hour at 07:30. Pause, change speed or drag the timeline.
  • Click a battalion, division, corps or mine for details. Events appear top right; click one to fly there.

This first version shows the start positions and the timetable. The battle itself (attacks going in, succeeding or failing, and the front lines moving) is the next stage. Trench lines are traced from 1916 trench maps and are approximate; battalion positions are placed along each division's frontage; the Montauban–Maricourt sector is drawn without a trench map.
